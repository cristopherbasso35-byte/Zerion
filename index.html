Our Story

Zerion was founded in 2026 by two guys; initially, they started it just for fun, but then built a serious vision and an obsession with proving that anyone can become something.

No sponsors, no infrastructure: just a desire to "grow" and the conviction that talent alone isn't enough without a method. Everything else grew from there—the Academy, the Performance Lab, the mental performance unit—all designed to take that very talent all the way to the stage.

Today, we are a full-fledged organization, but our goal remains the same: to grow and make a name for ourselves, by seeking out dedicated players and unique talent.
